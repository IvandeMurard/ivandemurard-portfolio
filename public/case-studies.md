## The Hospitality Multi-Agent Architecture

Five agents, each owning one job, talking only through MCP. Three have a page; the orchestrator and Peritia are design.

[Read the architecture note](https://ivandemurard.com/architecture)

### Aetherix

F&B agent · Status: Pre-pilot

The F&B agent for boutique hotels: one recommendation a day in WhatsApp, the manager's reply, the outcome recorded next to it.

Shows: product strategy, a loop measured against outcomes, evaluation gates.

[Read the case](https://ivandemurard.com/aetherix)

### Tacet

Environment agent · Status: Maintenance

Predicts how noise, weather and events around a hotel affect guests and revenue, and sends alerts to the RMS and PMS.

Shows: an agent exposed over MCP, then paused and labelled as such.

[Read the case](https://ivandemurard.com/tacet)

### Anima

Guest memory · Status: Compliance Gated

What a hotel knows about its guests sits in one person's head or in scattered tools, so needs are met late. Anima helps teams anticipate them and adapt the stay, from what guests chose to share, with less strain on operations.

Shows: privacy by design: consent, decay, and a data-protection gate before launch.

[Read the case](https://ivandemurard.com/anima)

## Standalone projects

Outside the architecture: the assistant of this site, the same thesis in aviation maintenance, a data product, and the company that came before all of it.

### Rose

The assistant of this site · Status: Live, text only

Rose answers questions about the page you are reading, from the site's own text.

Shows: an AI assistant shipped, evaluated and changed in public.

[Read the case](https://ivandemurard.com/rose)

### Lore

Aviation maintenance · Status: Evaluated

Captures the tacit knowledge a retiring technician never wrote down and returns it to a junior on the shop floor by voice, always behind the procedure.

Shows: a safety-first evaluation harness: 64 cases, pure-function graders.

[Read the case](https://ivandemurard.com/lore)

### Compass

Data product, Paris · Status: Partial

Every address has a story. Compass helps entrepreneurs choose premises that fit their project, with the context listings leave out.

Shows: a data product where every figure carries its source, licence and date.

[Read the case](https://ivandemurard.com/compass)

A two-year B2G SaaS I co-founded with three partners, to help cities reduce noise pollution with open data.

Shows: 0 to 1 with four co-founders, and knowing when to stop.
