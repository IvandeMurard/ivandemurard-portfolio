07 · Trust ● Built

Trust has two separate contracts.

One concerns data: what is retained and transmitted. The other concerns knowledge: what Rose may assert or infer. Mixing them would make privacy sound like proof, or proof sound like privacy.

Contract 1 · Data

What is retained and transmitted.

  • ●Your conversation stays in this tab for 60 minutes, or until a hard refresh.
  • ●Questions are stored with personal details masked by pattern, then purged after 90 days.
  • ●Booking opens the calendar and nothing else. No transcript travels with it.

Contract 2 · Knowledge

What Rose may assert or infer.

  • ●Rose answers from the same Markdown pages the MCP server reads, with one or two links to them.
  • ●A delivery status is preserved: planned is never turned into done, sandbox never into production.
  • ◐Released after 15 synthetic cases, one sample each, read by a person.

The answer rule ● Built

A correct answer can still be the wrong answer.

So Rose answers the decision behind the question first, keeps the evidence limit, and leaves implementation detail for when it is asked.

Contracts are only as good as the checks behind them.

08 · What she got wrong ◐ Validation

What she got wrong, and how I check her.

Many of Rose's rules started as a mistake caught in testing, before any visitor saw it. Three of them, from synthetic test runs in October.

  1. 24 Oct · release gate, 15 cases

    She said

    A follow-up suggestion about Sonor named a team member who does not exist.

    What I changed

    Rose may name only Ivan, plus names written word for word in the references.

    Result

    No invented names in the final run.

  2. 25 to 26 Oct · self-knowledge tests

    She said

    On her own page, asked how Ivan measures her (“What does Ivan measure?”), she answered with Aetherix figures.

    What I changed

    Repeating “stay on topic” did not work. I removed the competing material from her prompt and load only what the question needs.

    Result

    Passed on the next focused run. Self-knowledge turned out to be a reading problem: she had her facts, other material outweighed them.

  3. 26 Oct · self-knowledge tests

    She said

    On Aetherix, she invented precise error rates and attached a real figure to the wrong model.

    What I changed

    For the two high-precision questions on /aetherix, she returns an answer I wrote and checked. Elsewhere, every figure carries its model, metric and scope.

    Result

    Open-ended answers still under evaluation. That version is on hold.

How a case is judged

Each synthetic question is written before the run, with the behaviour it requires and the failure that would block release. Answers are read by a person, not graded by the model.

  • Accuracy
  • Context
  • Directness
  • Evidence
  • Visitor value
  • Judgment
  • Booking discipline
  • Trust and privacy
  • Verdict · Pass, fail or unclear, with the quoted answer as evidence.
  • Critical · An invented fact or a privacy slip fails the case. It is never averaged away.
  • Limit · One sample per case is not a reliability estimate, and usage logs are not proof of correctness.

Sources: docs/rose-p4-evaluation.md, rose-p5-verification.md, rose-stage-b-remediation.md, in the site repository.

The checks cover what she does today. Here is what comes next, and why it waits.

09 · Next steps ○ Design

Make the whole site easier to navigate, not just one reply.

Contextual assistant is the direction; adaptive reading is one possible behaviour inside it. Rose could locate, reformulate and visualise the relationships between pages while preserving the original sources and their limits. Rose remains text-based today.

  1. A complete, automatic semantic graph of the site The map is written by hand. It stays small and true before it grows.
  2. Diagrams that explain the complex parts A visual rewording of a dense passage, to teach rather than decorate. Drawn from a fixed set of diagrams I checked.
  3. Answers read aloud For readers who prefer to listen: a spoken version of a written answer, which stays the reference.
  4. Placing any passage in context Relating what you are reading to the rest of the page and the site, not only the passages I marked.

Some things I have not settled. I would rather say so.

10 · Open questions

Questions I am leaving open, rather than calling them features.

  1. 01 · Value

    Does a source-faithful answer help each reader understand the work from their own angle?

    Hypothesis
    One page every reader can question beats separate versions per audience, which failed in my previous portfolio.
    Signal
    The same question asked twice in one session, and what readers write in an optional feedback form.
    Decision
    If it holds, Rose gets more context and scope. If not, I narrow her to navigation.
    Cost of being wrong
    Rose adds noise to the content she was built to serve, and costs me credibility.

  2. 02 · Measure

    What should count as Rose helping, if booking is an outcome rather than the goal?

    Hypothesis
    Booking stays possible, but value comes first: Rose helps when readers use her and go to the sources.
    Signal
    Share of visits that open Rose, then answers whose source link is followed, page by page.
    Decision
    If few readers open her, I rethink how the dock invites a first question before adding any capability.
    Cost of being wrong
    Flattering numbers that hide how narrow the real use is.

  3. 03 · Role

    Should Rose remain an index of the site, or become a critical discussion partner?

    Hypothesis
    Rose is more useful when she connects content and questions my choices, never the reader’s, from trade-offs I documented.
    Signal
    Whether questions ask “where” or “why”, and whether answers link beyond the current page.
    Decision
    If it holds, she questions my choices from documented limits. If not, she returns to a stricter index.
    Cost of being wrong
    An invented critique costs more credibility than no critique at all.

  4. 04 · Modality

    When would voice reduce navigation work rather than add spectacle?

    Hypothesis
    Voice helps on long pages: a spoken summary while Rose shows the passage she is talking about.
    Signal
    Requests to summarise in the logs, then use of a simple Listen button before any custom voice.
    Decision
    If Listen gets used, I invest in a voice matching the site. If not, voice leaves the roadmap.
    Cost of being wrong
    A demonstrative feature, without use, to maintain.
