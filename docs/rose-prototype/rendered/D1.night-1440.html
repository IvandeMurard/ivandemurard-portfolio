00 · The idea

What if the portfolio could answer back?

A case study shows the work. It cannot anticipate every question someone brings to it. I built Rose to explore another way in: a conversation alongside the evidence.

● BUILT The first version shipped on 21 July 2026. Its brief was narrower than that possibility: help a visitor decide whether to book a conversation with me.

I named Rose after, and shaped her dock on, userose.ai's website agent. The idea of an agent commenting the page it lives on came from co-existence.ai. What I changed: her goal. Not selling. Explaining, reformulating, contextualising.

So the first version did one thing: qualify.

01 · 21 Jul 2026 ● Built

Qualify

The brief
Help the visitor decide, in two or three exchanges, whether to book 30 minutes with me.
The tension
Four intent chips asked visitors who they were before showing them anything.

What I decided

Ship a small dock with four intents and a booking card.

A qualification funnel with one destination.

Source: original prompt and config, commit 51245fc

One destination, and the same four boxes whatever page you were on. That was the next problem.

02 · 7 Aug 2026 ● Built

Listen to the page

The brief
Keep booking reachable.
The tension
The same four intents appeared on every page, whatever the visitor was reading.

What I decided

Replace fixed intents with three questions written for each page, and keep Book a call as a constant exit.

Questions start from the work, not from the visitor’s profile.

Source: archived plan, contextual starters, 7 Aug

The questions now started from the page. The answers did not, yet.

03 · 10 Aug 2026 ● Built

Know where you are

The brief
Answer generally about Ivan.
The tension
A page-specific question deserves a page-specific answer.

What I decided

Pass the current page and intent into Rose’s context.

Answers refer to the case study being read.

Source: commit b236ff3, page context and intent

Rose knew where you were. Where she should live was the next question.

04 · 14 Oct 2026 ● Built

One quiet dock

The brief
Several entry points to Rose.
The tension
A dock competing with the page reads as a sales widget.

What I decided

Unify the dock: discreet at rest, full contrast in use, questions by page.

Rose stays out of the way until asked.

Source: commit 7752dbb, unified dock and chips

Quiet until asked. Then: what happens to what you tell her?

05 · 24 Oct 2026 ● Built

Earn trust

The brief
Send the conversation with each booking request.
The tension
A transcript is more than a visitor agreed to share.

What I decided

Booking opens the calendar and nothing else.

Contact is an optional outcome, not the goal of every answer.

Source: privacy, memory and evaluation work, 24 Oct

Five decisions later, here is what she does today.

06 · What Rose can do now ○ Design

Ask about what you're reading.

Rose knows the page, the section in view and the passages I marked. She answers in the dock, in the format the question needs: a passage to jump to, plain words, a link to other work, or a diagram.

What she does is narrow on purpose. What she may keep, and say, is narrower still.
