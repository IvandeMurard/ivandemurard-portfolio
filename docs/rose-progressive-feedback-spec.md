# Rose progressive feedback, specification (not implemented)

Question: "Did Rose help you move your thinking forward?"

- Trigger: after two completed answers in one tab session. A new question is never read as a negative signal.
- Frequency: at most once per session (sessionStorage flag); never shown again after dismissal or submission in that session.
- Dismissible: one close control, no penalty, no repeat prompt.
- Answers: optional Yes / Partly / Not yet. Optional follow-up: What helped most? / What would make this more useful? / What was missing?
- Explicit submission: nothing is sent until the visitor presses Send.
- Privacy: no IP, user agent or fingerprinting; not joined to click sessions; email and phone patterns masked; 90-day purge; disclosed on /privacy.
- Use: Yes with deeper questions marks productive curiosity; Not yet marks content gaps to fix in the published pages; requests needing organisational context point to a human conversation.
