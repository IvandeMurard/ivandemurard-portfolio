## Tacet

URL: https://ivandemurard.com/tacet. Tacet is a headless Environmental Twin for hospitality (B2B, 2026, V3). It began as a Bruitparif-powered Score Sérénité dashboard for Parisians, then pivoted to a physics-based, software-only model of the property and its surroundings, exposed via an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server. Four architectural pillars: (A) a 3D ray-tracing physics engine built on shapely and osmnx that computes line of sight and building shielding to cut false positives; (B) a dual memory store with idiosyncratic learning per GPS coordinate plus a Hive Mind (Research: no network to aggregate yet) that would recalibrate baseline sensitivity across the network; (C) a human-in-the-loop guardrail that never changes prices on its own but formulates Price Modifier Rules (for example minus 15% on street-facing suites between June 12 and 14) for Revenue Manager approval; (D) conversational explainability through a mathematical Chain of Thought embedded in every JSON payload (base noise, distance attenuation, ray-tracing penalty, final impact). Stack: Python 3.12, FastAPI, Anthropic MCP SDK, SQLAlchemy, OSMnx, Shapely, GeoPandas, OAuth2, Mews Open API, Apaleo API, RMS webhooks. Designed to work in tandem with Aetherix: Aetherix consumes Tacet rules via MCP, providing internal context (occupancy, F&B, staffing) while Tacet provides external context (street, weather, events). Until the orchestrator is built (Design), this exchange is direct: the one documented exception, non-personal, read-only, logged. ESG impact: yield protection through pre-emptive pricing of noisy inventory, operational excellence through F&B and staffing buffers ahead of weather or social disruption, sustainability through proactive HVAC optimisation and noise mitigation. Source: https://github.com/IvandeMurard/tacet-app/blob/main/CASE_STUDY_V3.md.

## Lore

URL: https://ivandemurard.com/lore. Repository: https://github.com/IvandeMurard/Lore (tag v0.1-hackathon for the V1 build).

Lore is a voice AI mentor for aviation maintenance: a living memory for a trade that is handed down out loud. It captures what a retiring technician knows and never wrote down, then gives it back to a junior on the shop floor by voice. Origin: Speechmatics x AI Collective Paris hackathon, 28 Feb to 1 Mar 2026, V1 built in 24 hours by a team of four, developed solo since. Status: prototype, synthetic procedures, mock aircraft data, evaluation harness running in CI.

Three modes. Capture: the senior debriefs by voice after an intervention and Lore interviews back, filing knowledge against airframe, component and conditions. Query: the junior asks out loud and the answer arrives in three layers, in order, the relevant SOP excerpt, then the senior's oral knowledge attributed by name and month, then that specific aircraft's own history. Log: spoken work records go straight into the aircraft's memory, no keyboard.

Rule number one: Lore never contradicts a procedure, it completes it. Where a senior's habit and the manual disagree, the manual sets the limit and the senior's judgement is reported as context, attributed.

Thesis. Aetherix exists because hotels lose critical knowledge when a manager leaves; Lore exists because the same loss happens when a senior technician retires. What separates them is the cost of being wrong: in hospitality it compounds quietly, in maintenance a wrong figure touches airworthiness on the first occurrence.

Stack: Speechmatics real-time STT, OpenAI GPT-4o, Backboard (thread per aircraft and per technician), OpenAI TTS, Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind, Vercel.

## Anima

URL: https://ivandemurard.com/anima. Memory that means no guest has to ask twice. A guest memory node: keeps what a guest said and what happened, so nobody asks twice, without watching. It is the node that is not allowed to simply accumulate, a memory product with a deliberate limit, not a personalisation product.

Positioning. Anima is a guest memory node, not a twin. An employee can be told they are modelled, consulted and can refuse; a guest cannot meaningfully consent to being modelled during a two-night stay. The older version of this was the concierge who never had to ask twice, a guest memory running on one person's recall, with a bus factor of one, which is the same loss Lore addresses for technical expertise.

Four memory layers with different lifetimes. Working: the current stay, expires at check-out. Episodic: this stay and a short tail, weeks. Semantic: durable preferences stated or repeatedly confirmed, long with decay. Segment: anonymised patterns, aggregate and never individual. Temporal separation is the design: a guest ordering a decaf at 11pm before an early flight should be used tonight and never become a permanent belief.

Confidence. Nothing is stored as a fact. Every claim carries a weight, a source and an age, and weights fall unless renewed. A preference from 2019 nobody has confirmed does not outvote something said last week. A system that reports a confidence it did not measure is worse than one that reports none.

Ethics in the data model. Edmondo Grassi (The Remains of the Stay: Memory, Identity, and the Afterlife of the Guest, Hotel Yearbook 2026 Technology Edition) notes that the stay no longer ends at check-out, it merely changes form, and asks whether remembering is an act of care or an exercise of power. The answer here is decay on every claim, layers that expire by design, inference marked as inference, and a scope gate that makes total recall impossible rather than discouraged.

Boundaries. Any consumer declares which layer it queries; there is no call returning everything known about a person. Anima answers who a guest is right now and never what to do about it: that belongs to the orchestrator, with a human validating. Guest experience is not a node, it is a cross-cutting signal, which is why guest.* lives in the shared contract.

Status. Synthetic PoC. Four-layer memory implemented, working MCP server, evaluated on a synthetic cohort. Never run on a real guest, and it will not until a formal data-protection assessment is complete. Shipped as a prototype at an Anthropic hackathon, June 2026. Production is phase 4.

## Compass

Standalone projects: Compass is a standalone data product, not a node, agent or part of the Hospitality Multi-Agent Architecture.
Six top-level sections: One address, read in context; Product strategy (The problem, The bet, What it refuses, Against what exists); Where AI sits; Built for people and AI agents; How sure is it?; Where it stands.

URL: https://ivandemurard.com/compass. Live app: https://paris-compass.lovable.app. Repository: https://github.com/IvandeMurard/paris-compass. MCP server: npx -y paris-compass-mcp, published on npm and the MCP registry.

Every address has a story. Compass reads it before you sign. Open data turned into traceable, address-level context for commercial location decisions in Paris. APUR BDCom 2023 shopfront census · BODACC legal notices · INSEE Sirene · 85,418 units · every figure with its source, licence and date. Earlier census records and figures derived from them remain withheld until their licence is cleared. It refuses a single score out of 100, a rent estimate, brokers as users, and any figure without a cited public source. Compass is agent-ready: its published, read-only MCP server exposes six tools, and an AI agent gets the same figures, sources and confidence levels as a person sees on the map. AI stays out of the deterministic scoring core; it works above it, reading legal notices and matching sales to shopfronts (Design, planned for Q4 2026). Status: Corpus and evaluation live, MCP server published, web demo on a frozen sample (17th arrondissement), demo on the live corpus in progress.
