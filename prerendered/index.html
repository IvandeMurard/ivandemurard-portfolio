    03Approach

    My approach

    Three convictions that shape every product I build, and the method that follows from them.

    Human interactions first

    Technology should give time back to people, not pull them deeper into screens. The best automation is the one that lets a host be more present.

    Real business value

    Reducing operational costs, freeing capacity, sharpening decisions. Every product I ship has to move a number that matters to the business.

    Full-stack 0→1 craft

    From insight to shipped product, bridging digital interfaces and physical operations, so the experience holds together end to end.

    In practice: read the ground first, turn that reading into shipped product decisions, and pick the lightest mechanism that solves the job, with the human always in the loop. The track record behind it.
