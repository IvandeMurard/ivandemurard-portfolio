    Whose truth is the ground truth?

    An AI product is only as honest as the answer it learns from. Choosing that answer is a product decision, made before the data arrives.

    Read withChatGPTClaudedescriptionRaw markdown

    person_searchRead this if
    you own an AI product that recommends, decides or acts.
    targetWhat it gives you
    name the truth each step returns, who holds it, when, how it can be gamed, and what it is worth.
    scienceTested on
    two products, one that gets a fact back the next day, one that never does. Gaps stated.
    What you will decide differently
    1. 01**"Truth" is plural.** Documented, declared, observed, counterfactual: each has an owner, a delay and a way to fool you.
    2. 02**A recommendation is a claim.** Nobody knows it was right when it is sent.
    3. 03**A "yes" is not a "right".** Optimise acceptance and the product learns to say what is easy to approve.
    4. 04**Some truth never comes back.** The plan not taken. The signed record in a hangar.
    5. 05**Truth is an asset.** Inside, it is memory that does not leave with the manager. Outside, it is the only basis for pricing on results.
    6. 06**Write the verdict before the data.** Aetherix's thresholds are public and frozen. None of its four tests has run yet.

    1. What "truth" means herelink

    helpSEC. 1

    Truth is the answer a product is judged by, or learns from. In a live product there are several, and they disagree.

    In practiceOne Aetherix recommendation receives three different answers in two days.

    FIG. 2 · Each kind of truth has an owner, a delay and a way to fool you.
    KindDefinitionHeld byArrivesHow it fools you
    Documentedwhat is written as correct before anything happens: a procedure, a case setregulator, teambeforeonly as right as the document
    Declaredwhat a person says about the output: accept, modify, ignorethe usersame daymeasures agreement, not correctness
    Observedwhat actually happened, measuredthe till, the systemlatersays what happened, not what should have
    Counterfactualwhat the option not taken would have producednobodynevercan be modelled, never seen
    • —Not interchangeable. Learn from declared truth and the product learns to please. Learn only from documented truth and it learns to recite.
    • —The product decision: which truth counts at which step, and which one the system may learn from without a human.

    2. A Tuesday, station by stationlink

    calendar_todaySEC. 2

    Abstract truths become concrete on one ordinary service.

    In practiceAn illustrative breakfast. The figures are invented, in the format of Aetherix's daily message.

    FIG. 3 · The same recommendation, judged four ways. Illustrative figures.
    WhenWhat happensTruthWhat it tells youWhat it does not
    Monday eveningAetherix: 84 covers, 4 front-of-house staffnone yetthe claimwhether it is right
    Monday eveningThe manager replies "3 is enough"declared: modifiedthe manager disagrees on staffingwho was right
    Tuesday morning79 covers are servedobservedthe forecast was 6% highwhether 3 staff was enough
    NeverTuesday with 4 staffcounterfactual-whether the service lost anything
    • —The forecast gets a clean answer: 6% high. It can be learned from.
    • —The staffing gets no clean answer. Service went fine with 3? Nobody measured it. The disagreement is recorded, not settled.
    • —What a product can still do: price the two mistakes. Short-staffing and over-staffing do not cost the same, so a recommendation can say which way it leans and why. Status: Synthetic PoC, nothing measured on real data.

    3. A "yes" is not a "right"link

    thumb_upSEC. 3

    The easiest number to collect is the easiest to game.

    In practiceAcceptance rate.

    • —The trap (Goodhart's law): when a measure becomes a target, it stops measuring. A product optimised on acceptance learns to send recommendations that cost nothing to approve.
    • —From Aetherix's own data: the forecast beats "the last four Tuesdays" by 0.6 points on the typical day. A product that simply repeated last Tuesday would be accepted often and teach nothing.
    • —The counterweight: read acceptance with manager minutes per accepted recommendation. Watch ignored hardest: an override engages, an ignore routes around.

    4. The truth that never comes backlink

    flightSEC. 4

    Some answers are structurally missing.

    In practiceLore, in aviation maintenance.

    • —The signed work record never returns to the product.
    • —Its truth is a hierarchy of authority: the procedure wins any conflict, by rule. Where the procedure is silent, the product abstains.
    • —The cost: effort moves before release. Close to a third of Lore's code is its evaluation harness, because nothing downstream will correct it.
    FIG. 4 · Where the answer comes from when the world does not reply.
    QuestionAetherix (answer returns)Lore (answer never returns)
    What is the reference?real covers, next daythe procedure, by rule
    Who arbitrates a conflict?the observed outcomethe manual, then abstention
    Where does the effort go?the outcome loopthe pre-release harness
    What may it learn alone?a calibrated forecast, once measurednothing; a human certifies

    5. What truth is worth to a businesslink

    paymentsSEC. 5

    Recorded truth is an asset, and so is the discipline of not overstating it.

    In practiceAetherix's choice to price on results.

    FIG. 5 · Inside, truth is memory. Outside, it is proof.
    UseNeedsStatus in Aetherix
    InsideA record of recommendation, reply and outcome: memory that does not leave with the managerobserved truth, captured every dayrecord Built on sandbox data
    InsideCalibration: when the product says 80%, it is right 80% of the timeobserved truth over timeSynthetic PoC
    OutsidePricing on resultsan observed truth both sides acceptstated strategy, no client yet
    OutsideProof for a buyer, a partner, a marketplaceoutcomes measured against a rule fixed in advanceprotocol published, tests not run
    OutsidePatterns shared across hotelsanonymised outcomes from many propertiesResearch
    • —Pricing on results is impossible without an agreed truth. Declared truth will not do: the payer would be the judge.
    • —Certify the method now, the truth later. Before outcomes exist, a product can prove how it measures: replayable cases, abstention, no contradiction. Proof of value needs the outcome loop.
    • —Ownership is a contract question. Who owns a hotel's outcome history, and what may leave the property, is decided before the first pilot, not after.

    6. Write the verdict before the datalink

    gavelSEC. 6

    Choosing the measure after seeing the result is how products flatter themselves.

    In practiceAetherix's proof protocol, frozen before any of its tests ran.

    FIG. 6 · Four links, four thresholds, none tested yet. No result is DARK, not green.
    LinkQuestionPassFailToday
    ModelBeats "the last four same weekdays"?gap ≥ 5 pointsgap < 2 pointsDARK, not started
    DecisionWould it change a real decision?≥ 3 of 5 managers≤ 1 of 5DARK, not started
    AdoptionDo managers act on it?≥ 30% acceptance< 30%DARK, needs a pilot
    EconomicsMargin on the cost of service?≥ 70% at 10 properties< 70%WARN, passes on estimates
    • —A fail is published. On the model or decision link it triggers a written pivot note within five working days.
    • —Honest scale: the earlier public benchmark gap was 0.6 points. Not covered by the protocol; it would sit in the fail band.

    7. Checklistlink

    • —Name, for each step, the truth that comes back, who holds it and when.
    • —Decide which truth the product may learn from without a human.
    • —Never read acceptance alone.
    • —Settle who owns the outcome history before the first pilot.
    • —Freeze pass and fail thresholds before the first result. Publish the fails.

    8. FAQlink

    Isn't the user always right?link

    About what they want, yes. Whether the product was correct is answered later, by something else.

    What if the truth never comes back?link

    Then the product does not learn on its own there. A rule arbitrates, a human certifies, effort moves before release.

    Can a company sell its verified know-how?link

    It can prove its method today. It can only sell results once outcomes are measured against a rule both sides accepted in advance.
