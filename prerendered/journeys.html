    The Hospitality Multi-Agent Architecture

    Aetherix

    The F&B agent for boutique hotels. One message a day in WhatsApp, the manager's reply, and the outcome recorded next to it. Built. Running on sandbox and public data. No hotel connected yet.

    The food and beverage agent of the architecture.

    Tacet

    The Synthetic Acoustic Twin. A headless intelligence layer for hotels: a physics-based, software-only acoustic model that predicts how construction, traffic, weather and events affect guest rooms, then pushes JSON alerts to RMS and PMS for proactive yield management.

    The environmental agent of the architecture.

    Anima

    The guest memory node, and the one that is not allowed to simply accumulate. Four memory layers with different lifetimes, claims that carry a weight and a source and decay unless renewed, and a scope gate that makes asking for a whole person impossible. Built, evaluated on synthetic data, and deliberately not switched on.

    The guest memory node.

    Standalone projects

    Not part of this architecture. Lore is the same architecture under a stricter regime, in aviation maintenance. Sonor is the two-year B2G product that came before all of it. Compass reads commercial premises in Paris from public open data.

    Standalone project

    Lore

    A living memory for a trade that is handed down out loud. Lore captures what a retiring aviation technician knows and never wrote down, then gives it back to a junior on the shop floor by voice, always behind the procedure, graded by a harness that treats safety as a tier with no budget.

    Outside: the same architecture in aviation maintenance, where a licensed technician signs the release.

    Standalone project

    Sonor

    Reclaiming urban silence through data. As Co-founder and Product Lead, I built a SaaS platform that turns open data into actionable acoustic intelligence for cities. Used by municipalities to protect citizen health, drive sustainability policies, and increase territorial attractiveness.

    Outside: a B2G SaaS, two years, before any of this.
