    Case Study: Sonor (2020-2022)

    Sonor: The Quiet Art of
    Urban Intelligence.

    B2G that turned acoustic into a decision tool for cities. Taken from discovery to MVP, technical core validated; stopped in 2022 after discovery concluded no contract would clear.

    Role

    Co-founder and Product Lead

    Duration

    2020 to 2022

    Team

    4 co-founders, plus a developer and data scientist

    Context

    Incubated at Matrice (Banque des Territoires)

    Funding

    €20k in grants

    Outcome

    MVP shipped, commercial discussions with municipalities, no contract

    Original project site (2020)arrow_outward

    The Hypothesis

    Our first 2020 prototype

    The vision: a loop between a city and its residents. The platform gathers information (noise cartography, tracking of field surveys and regulation, alerts left by residents), field surveys collect what residents experience, findings are reported to the city, and the city's actions are communicated back to residents.

    The Product

    From prototype to MVP.

    What it is

    A SaaS platform built to turn open noise data into cartography and recommendations for city teams: map exposure, prioritise interventions, justify policy with evidence.

    A loop between a city and its residents

    Residents leave alerts and field surveys collect what they experience. Findings are reported to the city, and the city's actions are communicated back to residents.

    What happened

    To ease adoption, we offered services around the platform. Each city asked for something different: a field feasibility study for a Paris district town hall, mediation and a field study for a town west of Paris, communication on actions taken for a metropolitan authority. We had commercial discussions; none became a contract.

    Why it stopped

    Discovery concluded no contract would clear. When the grants and the incubation ended, we stopped Sonor.

    What I'd change

    Stay on delivering the product. We drifted into a "neo-agency" answering every need a city expressed, so the loop lived in our team's time, not in the product. I believe one use case, delivered and repeated, would have had a better chance of a first contract. I cannot prove it.

    What I applied next

    Aetherix starts with one job, F&B covers, because its result can be checked the next morning. And the loop lives in the product: the recommendation, the manager's reply and the outcome are recorded without anyone in between.

    Related work

    Tacet carries the acoustic thesis forward, as an environmental twin for boutique hotels.
