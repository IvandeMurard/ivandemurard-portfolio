    Where I come from

    I started in agri-food business, passionate about food and where it came from, and working in hospitality. In 2017, I win my first team innovation competition, since then curiosity led me to explore various topics, environments, and organizations, always with the same drive: build meaningful, lasting products.

    Lately, I worked two years co-founding Sonor, a 0 to 1 open data SaaS for urban noise mapping, backed by Matrice and La Banque des Territoires, which taught me how to read a field, build with very little, and ship something cities actually used. Every role since has been a different angle on the same craft.

    Principles

    What I build with

    trending_up

    Real business value

    Every product I ship has to move a number that matters: operational cost down, capacity freed, decisions sharpened. If it doesn't, it's a demo.

    favorite

    Human interactions first

    Technology should give time back to people, not pull them deeper into screens. The best automation is the one that lets a host be more present.

    architecture

    Full-stack 0 to 1 craft

    From insight to shipped product, bridging digital interfaces with physical operations, so the experience holds together end to end.

    Method
    Method

    How I work

    A loop, not a waterfall. Four moves I keep returning to, on every project.

    01

    Problem analysis

    I start by understanding context, problem and architecture before writing a line. Field-level fluency before design.

    02

    Build

    I pick the lightest stack and methodology that solves the job, then iterate fast on real usage, not demos.

    03

    Continuous discovery

    I keep challenging the vision and roadmap by reading the market, the competition, and the weak signals.

    04

    Documenting

    I keep a single source of truth, so context, decisions, and learnings stay accessible to everyone who follows.
