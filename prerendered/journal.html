    1. 1

      Lore answers that the reading is "considered NORMAL" and that "no further action is required". Fluent, and wrong.

    2. 2

      The first harness passed it. Reading the answers caught it; the graders now fail it and block the release.

      The code now holds the rule: when Lore contradicts the manual, the manual's verdict ships.

    For deciding what your product learns from

    Whose truth is the ground truth?

    A forecast is a claim, a manager's "yes" is a preference, real covers are a fact the next day. Which one your product learns from decides what it becomes.

    A yes is not a right

    A manager can approve a wrong forecast and reject a right one. A product that repeated last Tuesday's numbers would be approved often and teach nothing.
